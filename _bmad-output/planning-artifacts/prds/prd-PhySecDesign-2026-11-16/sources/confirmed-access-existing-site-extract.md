## Four consistency risks to address during integration

1. **Analysis population versus visual layers.** The explicit Current installation/Proposed design basis may change eligible assets and calculations. Hiding a layer or DORI overlay must still remain presentation-only under FR-12/FR-14. Label basis, assumptions and exclusions in results/schedules; do not double-count replacements, turn unknown inputs into zero demand, or infer that an asset with no purchase cost has no capacity demand. A known failed asset receiving no functioning coverage credit does not by itself establish zero physical power/port demand.
2. **Planned actions versus actual installation.** Relocate, Remove and Replace describe intended work before they describe completed facts. Preserve current positions/equipment until actual field changes are recorded; archive a predecessor after recorded removal. Planning must not alter a surveyed baseline or manufacture installation/configuration/test evidence. Preserve device identity across relocation/service and use linked new IDs for physical replacements.
3. **Approvals, scoped tests and honest closeout.** Reviewer links and fixed-version acceptance do not add blocking approval gates. Inventorying unchanged assets does not mandate complete recommissioning, but selecting a narrow new-work scope cannot silently conceal known defects, dispose of issues, or satisfy required checks. Keep existing Completed/Closed with outstanding items semantics; unresolved applicability and out-of-scope disposition remain OQ-3/OQ-4/OQ-5.
4. **OQ-1 resolution versus OQ-2 uncertainty.** The accepted access model defines who may act on which project/revision and the POC data boundary. It does not settle how saved changes propagate, concurrent edits conflict, shared undo works, or durable storage is implemented. Update stale OQ-1 references in FR-2/NFR-5 and addendum source labels without declaring OQ-2 accepted or claiming current local review storage already satisfies shared editing.
