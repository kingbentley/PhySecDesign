### Earlier timing candidates — to be re-evaluated for the larger fixture

The timing table below preserves earlier candidate thresholds for historical context. The later Confirmed performance and analysis-accuracy targets section records the accepted current goals. This older table is historical and does not add its photo/PDF/report/backup candidates to the confirmed goals. It is not the accepted larger-site envelope: the 20-view/100-edge/100 ×100m case does not represent up to 60 physical cameras per plan, and heads/views or plan geometry may increase work further. Define and measure the actual larger workload before deciding thresholds; do not extrapolate the earlier 3-second result target or silently lower fidelity.

Use named test hardware (including an ordinary 8 GB RAM Windows laptop) and a repeatable online connection, targeting at least 20 Mbps down/5 Mbps up and no more than 100 ms service round-trip latency. Record actual conditions, builds, dataset bytes, cold/warm state and results. For ordinary repeated operations, at least 19 of 20 trials should meet the target; retain outliers and all errors. Measure long jobs at least three times, each within its stated bound. These are POC acceptance thresholds, not an availability SLA.

| Operation | Proposed target and measurement boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Local selection/control feedback | Within 250 ms; analysis can complete separately while controls remain usable. |
| Metadata edit saved | Durable acknowledgement within 2 seconds after committing an ordinary edit; attachment-bearing saves remain pending until associated files actually persist. |
| Shared update | Visible on the other connected client within 5 seconds of durable save. |
| Earlier small-fixture coverage/zone candidate | Final selected-plan combined coverage and all its zones (up to all 10 fixture zones on that plan) within 3 seconds after committing a camera/barrier/zone change, using the stated area/view/edge bounds and effective spacing no coarser than 1 m. |

Record live geocoder/map/provider latency separately from application processing; failures still need clear UI and preserved state, and mocked provider results alone do not establish the live map demonstration. Never show Saved before durable completion or report complete analysis/export while it is pending. If benchmark results miss a proposed target, investigate or explicitly revisit that target; do not silently lower geometry resolution or omit evidence to pass. The current combined-analysis work budget would permit only about 990 cells (roughly 3.2 m spacing over 100 × 100 m) at the worst-case 20 views/100 relevant edges. The proposed 1 m/3-second fixture therefore requires implementation improvements or an explicit acceptance-scope revision; it is not established by the existing solver.

Check calculation fixtures against independently computed expected values: equipment/cable/labor totals at displayed currency precision, known cable components/allowance applied once, bandwidth/storage/PoE units and compatible bases, and best-single-view DORI. For simple supported rectilinear fixtures, target modeled FoV/threshold distances within 1% and sampled zone area within 2 percentage points of an independently calculated reference at a documented sufficiently fine spacing. Record sampling/boundary uncertainty; use full computed values for threshold decisions so display rounding cannot turn a shortfall into a pass. These tolerances validate supported software fixtures, not field imaging performance, panoramic calibration or certification.

### File, package and recovery candidates — larger-site budgets still open

The earlier file figures below remain unapproved historical candidates. The later Proposed file, export, backup and recovery scope is the current recommendation; none of these older byte/page/recovery figures is automatically adopted. They must be re-budgeted against the larger property’s 20–30 plans, evidence volume, exact retained packages and full backup; they must not be advertised as adequate or imposed as permanent limits for that site. Any proposed multi-volume export or background-processing approach needs explicit UX/architecture treatment while preserving complete package scope and history.

Earlier proposed guards: a source PDF up to 50 MiB; an input image/photo up to 20 MiB before processing; retained plan/photo media up to 50 MiB in aggregate, including up to 100 photos. Retain usable processed previews, not an implied original-resolution archive; disclose resolution/quality changes. Start with the existing 3,200-pixel selected-plan-page render bound and six photos per note. Counts and per-file maxima cannot all be filled independently of the aggregate budget. Prepare a manifest that fits the combined fixture—for example four retained plans at up to 3 MiB each plus 100 processed photos averaging at most 0.35 MiB consume at most 47 MiB. Verify readability; do not damage required evidence quality just to fit. Reject unsupported/oversized/corrupt input with a clear reason while retaining the previous saved design.

Target complete reports up to 100 pages and 80 MiB final PDF bytes, with two photos per page initially. The byte guard refers to the final file, not the existing JPEG-payload counter. Test readable labels, continuation sheets, required evidence, failure reporting and partial/omission labels. A limit cannot justify presenting a silently shortened document as complete.

Target a complete desktop backup of up to 500 MiB including current records, retained revisions/evidence, processed media and exact issued/closeout package files. Four maximum-size PDFs plus the aggregate media budget use 370 MiB, leaving 130 MiB for records/history/container overhead; fixtures must satisfy all budgets together. The current 100 MiB JSON/duplicated-snapshot mechanism is not evidence that this works. Storage/container design is architecture work; it must preserve all required content within the tested budget or the budget must be explicitly revisited. Do not silently drop old PDFs or captured history. Source PDFs never retained and live credentials are not invented backup content.

Require acknowledged records/attachments to survive browser closure and an ordinary service restart with no lost acknowledged changes. In addition, propose an automatic complete daily recovery backup plus a backup before migrations/destructive maintenance, retaining at least seven daily generations independently of the live store. This backup rotation does not purge revision/evidence history inside the project. For catastrophic store loss, the POC recovery objective is the latest successful backup, no more than 24 hours old, restored within 60 minutes once the documented recovery environment is available. Backup age/failure must be visible; this is distinct from zero-loss ordinary restart behavior and not a production uptime commitment.

Demonstrate restore in an isolated environment: verify stable IDs/relationships, record and attachment counts, representative content, unresolved issues, archives and byte-identical retained PDFs/manifests. Do not reactivate old bearer links or restore credentials from a portable file; configure access through the existing access policy. Failed/incomplete backup or restore must not overwrite the live project. Export/download success alone is insufficient recovery evidence.
