# PRD Quality Review — Site Studio PRD, amendment 1.1 finalization

Reviewed 2026-11-18 against `.agents/skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-validation-checklist.md`. Scope: all of `prd.md` (730 lines), the addendum section "Proposed amendment 1.1 — reference-based additions" plus the addendum sections it touches, and `amendment-1.1-status.md`. Line numbers refer to `prd.md` unless a file is named. Counts: 0 critical, 2 high, 9 medium, 6 low.

## Overall verdict

**Gate: amendment 1.1 is not ready to be marked final as written, but nothing in it is structurally broken; two high findings and a handful of medium text-level contradictions need fixing first.** The 1.0 body still holds up: the thesis (honest, history-preserving records from design through closeout and return) is intact, the new requirements are specific and mostly carry strong worked Checks (FR-64's arithmetic is correct, FR-63's counted-versus-reachable rule is clear). The risk sits at the seams: the acceptance profile and success measures never mention FR-63–65, the new "nothing is blocked" Check in FR-30 leaves open whether a not-installed mark still prevents Completed, the device state model's derived-membership table disagrees with its own prose, and the PRD's summary of the amendment understates what changed in FR-55.

## Decision-readiness — adequate

Decisions are stated as decisions, and the amendment is unusually candid about where it departs from its sources (addendum.md line 812 lists three deliberate departures from the reliability-test reference). FR-63's governing choice, that only the home position is counted, is stated with what was given up (reach earns no credit). The weakness is the PRD's own account of the amendment. A reader who relies on lines 18 and 714 to learn what changed in the 1.0 baseline gets an incomplete answer, and the one place 1.0 behaviour was actually changed is not named there.

### Findings

- **medium** Amendment summary says the baseline was kept everywhere, but FR-55's cancellation scope changed (lines 18, 714; addendum.md line 824) — Line 714: "Where that session decided something this baseline also decides, this baseline stands: Kevin reviewed the differences on 2026-11-18 and kept this baseline on all of them. He adopted four clarifications". Line 18: "FR-1–62, NFR-1–6 and SM-1–5 keep their IDs and meaning." But `amendment-1.1-status.md` line 57 records a sixth decision, "A narrow exception to A3 is adopted", and FR-55 (line 585) now says "The same cancellation, with a reason, is available for an unissued proposal that is not eligible for Delete draft". Under 1.0, Cancel applied only to an issued or approved proposal. Line 714 lists the mistaken-mark reversal for FR-55 but not this exception; addendum.md line 824 repeats "adopted four clarifications". FR-22 (restore limited to design content) and FR-8/41 also changed observable behaviour, so "keep their ... meaning" overstates. *Fix:* In lines 18 and 714 and addendum.md line 824, name the cancellation exception as a fifth adopted change, and replace "keep their IDs and meaning" with "keep their IDs; FR-8, FR-22, FR-30, FR-37, FR-41 and FR-55 are clarified as listed".

- **medium** "No known product question remains open" while known unsettled items live only in the status note (line 712) — Line 712: "No known product question remains open." `amendment-1.1-status.md` records three items as unsettled: line 111 "Until it does, FR-63–65 are verified only by their own Checks"; line 167 "FR-64 calls the parts of a reliability period “stages”, while the Terms section of PRD 1.0 also uses “stages”"; line 168 "“Return to service” is used in two senses in both documents; neither settles it." *Fix:* Either resolve them (see the acceptance and terminology findings below) or list them in the Decision status section as accepted open items with an owner.

- **low** The condition default has a consequence that is not surfaced (lines 106, 110) — "It applies while the device is Existing and starts as Unknown" and "Condition is not changed automatically by a test outcome", with "Unknown condition is disclosed as an assumption under FR-13". Taken together, every device installed and fully passed in a scope carries Unknown condition, and a disclosed analysis assumption, until someone sets Working by hand. The model also does not say whether an Unknown-condition camera is credited in a current operational estimate (only Faulty is addressed). *Fix:* State the intended treatment of Unknown in current estimates, and say whether the Unknown-after-passing-tests outcome is intended or whether the contradiction warning should also cover condition versus test outcome.

## Substance over theater — strong

No persona, vision or NFR theater; the journeys are explicitly "not additional customer research or invented personas" (line 43), and NFRs carry thresholds. The amendment's content is earned: each of FR-63–65 has concrete rules and a worked scenario, and the trade-term table is labelled "for recognition only" (line 72). The reference section is honest about what was read ("Only pages Kevin shared were read", addendum.md line 801) and claims no compliance. No findings.

## Strategic coherence — adequate

The 1.0 thesis survives. FR-63 serves it directly (modeled coverage must not overclaim; reach "is never counted as covered", line 348). FR-64 reuses the check/attempt/issue rules instead of adding a parallel mechanism. FR-65 is the furthest from the stated arc: warranty terms and service-visit logs come from a textbook's maintenance chain (line 722: "Their findings informed FR-63–65"), not from the workflow Kevin described in UJ-1–4, and they edge toward maintenance management that line 569 then has to fence off. That is a defensible product choice, but the journeys and measures were not updated to show where it fits.

### Findings

- **high** The acceptance profile and success measures do not cover FR-63–65 (lines 647–665, 667–681, 708; addendum.md lines 676–704, 730–740) — The Functional coverage row (line 652) lists "multiple floors, cameras/other equipment, an obstruction, a targeted zone, routes, costs/capacity and survey evidence"; the Larger property row (line 653) has "150 physical cameras" with no pan/tilt/zoom share; SM-1–5 (lines 661–665) name no pan/tilt/zoom camera, reliability run, warranty or service visit. The addendum fixture (addendum.md line 698) lists "conventional and multi-head cameras, an explicitly unassessed panoramic view" and its five lifecycle steps (lines 700–704) contain no reliability run, warranty or service-visit step. The only timed analysis row (line 679) is "Combined visibility/DORI and all targeted zones on the busiest active plan"; FR-63's reach layer, per-location achievable detail and per-zone reach figures (lines 348–350) have no timing target, no statement that they are outside the 10-second row, and no reference-accuracy rule under NFR-4 (line 639). Meanwhile line 708 claims "Acceptance covers the FRs and NFRs within the agreed fixture/limits". The status note admits the gap (line 111) but the PRD does not. *Fix:* Add to the Functional coverage row at least one pan/tilt/zoom camera with a home position, a second stored position and a reachable zone; one reliability run with a counted outage; and one warranty plus a Start service visit cycle. State the pan/tilt/zoom count in the larger fixture (or that it is zero). Add a row or sentence saying whether reach is inside the 10-second combined-analysis target or may complete separately as Updating, and whether reach detail falls under the NFR-4 1% reference rule. Extend SM-3 or SM-1 to name the service-scope path, and mirror this in addendum.md lines 698–704.

- **low** UJ-4 and the scope table do not mention the service-visit path (lines 26–36, 48) — FR-65 adds a second way to return to a property ("a single Start service visit action reopens the project", line 567), but UJ-4 still describes only planned later work and SM-1 is defined as UJ-1–4. *Fix:* Add one clause to UJ-4, or state that a service visit is a variant of UJ-4.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate (thin within the amendment)

The 1.0 Checks remain concrete. Of the new requirements, FR-64 is the best specified: the Check's numbers work (57 of 60 minutes is 95%, below the 99% target, so Fail) and every stop/restart case has an outcome. FR-63 and FR-65 have good happy-path Checks but leave stated fallback rules untested. The serious gaps are where new sentences meet 1.0 closeout and estimate rules.

### Findings

- **high** FR-30's new Check says "nothing is blocked", leaving undefined whether a not-installed mark prevents Completed, and whether a scope can complete with a device still Proposed (lines 133, 503 versus 67, 89, 122, 541) — New text at line 503: "A device still marked not installed whose required installation checks have all passed shows the contradiction warning, and nothing is blocked." Line 133: "The warning blocks nothing and changes no outcome." But 1.0 says an outstanding item includes "Unfinished in-scope work" (line 67), Completed means "No unfinished in-scope work" (line 89), and FR-32's Check says "An unfinished installation ... prevents Completed" (line 541); SM-3 (line 663) treats "not installed" as a distinct status. The state model also makes the mark the only thing that advances the lifecycle: "Recording installation done advances the lifecycle ... an Add new device becomes Existing" (line 122), while phase progress is derived only from checks (line 128). Two readings follow and neither is stated. If the mark counts as unfinished work, then "nothing is blocked" is false for closeout. If it does not, a scope can close as Completed with an Add new device whose checks all passed but whose lifecycle is still Proposed, so it is absent from Current installation membership (line 130) and from "the latest installation" that the next scope starts from (line 555). *Fix:* State in the device state model and FR-32 whether Work recorded "not done" on a Work now item is an outstanding item. If it is, reword line 503 to "the warning itself blocks nothing; the not-installed mark remains unfinished work under FR-32". Add the case to FR-32's Check.

- **medium** The state model's membership table contradicts its own prose on corrected and deleted records (lines 112, 130–131) — Line 112: "Cancelled and corrected records are excluded from current totals and calculations". Line 130 defines Current installation membership as "Existing devices at their recorded locations", with no exclusion, and line 131 defines Proposed design membership as "Existing devices without a planned Remove or Replace ... and Proposed devices that are not cancelled". An Existing record corrected as a duplicate under FR-55 therefore remains in both populations by the table, and a deleted draft is not excluded from Proposed. The underlying cause is that the three record-ending outcomes (line 112) are described as actions but are not an axis in the "Stored on the device" table (lines 102–106), so the derived rows have nothing to reference. *Fix:* Add a record-status axis (for example Active, Deleted draft, Cancelled, Corrected) to the stored table and make both membership rows say "active records only".

- **medium** FR-22's "design content only" does not say which side as-installed device attributes, planned actions and stored positions fall on (lines 203, 205) — Design content is "proposed devices, planned positions, routes, zones, obstructions, annotations and settings"; facts are "Recorded actual positions, recorded installation and configuration work, evidence, test attempts, issues, cancellations, corrections and archive state". "settings" is unqualified. An installed camera's recorded aim, lens setting and height (changed in the field under FR-27, line 462), its pan/tilt/zoom stored positions and limits (a "meaningful change" on an installed camera, line 352), Current installation port assignments (FR-51, line 368), and the per-scope planned action, scope category and estimate are in neither list. The Check (line 205) covers only position and removal. *Fix:* Define the split by the model's own terms, for example "restore changes Proposed design content and never Current installation values", and name planned actions, scope category, estimate lines and stored positions explicitly. Add a re-aimed installed camera to the Check.

- **medium** FR-65's Check asserts an unchanged estimate in the one scenario where FR-17/40 would add a line (lines 565, 571 versus 238, 402) — Line 565: "a part that is itself a replaced device follows FR-36 and FR-53. Parts used and time spent are records only: they change no estimate". The Check (line 571) uses "a replaced power supply" and then "The estimate is unchanged." Power supplies are catalog devices (line 215: "low-voltage power supplies"). A replaced device is a planned Replace with "New proposed device ID linked to its predecessor, with purchase and applicable removal/installation cost" (line 238), and FR-17 estimates "new/replacement equipment" for the active scope (line 402). The Check therefore either contradicts those rules or silently treats this power supply as a non-device part. *Fix:* Change the Check's part to something that is not a catalog device (a fuse or connector), or state that a device replaced during a service visit creates a replacement record whose estimate line is optional in a service scope, and cite FR-40 beside FR-36/53.

- **medium** FR-63's Check tests only the happy path, and its last sentence is looser than the rule (lines 346–356) — Stated behaviours with no testable consequence: "Until a home position is defined, the camera contributes no assessed coverage" (line 346); "Without sufficient optical data, show the reach outline alone with detail Not assessed"; "Where the pan or tilt limits are unknown, show no reach"; "Near a limit, where only a wider view can include the location, show the detail of that wider view" (line 348); and "a proposed edit updates the design without flagging field checks" (line 352). It is also not stated whether a placed camera with no per-camera limits uses the model's range or counts as unknown ("Each placed camera can carry its own pan and tilt limits within the model’s range. Unknown ranges and limits stay unknown", line 344). The Check's "Changing the home position changes counted coverage and flags that position’s checks" (line 356) omits the installed-only condition of line 352, and does not distinguish editing the home position's values from designating a different stored position as home, where no position's settings changed. *Fix:* Add Check clauses for a camera with no home position, with unknown limits, and with missing optics; say that unset per-camera limits default to the model range; and qualify the final clause with "on the installed camera" and the re-designation case.

- **medium** FR-18's subsystem rule cannot be satisfied for lines that have no equipment type (lines 410–414) — "Assign each line to one subsystem by default from its equipment type" and the Check "Subsystem subtotals sum to the same total as the ungrouped estimate". Percentage lines whose base spans categories ("A 10% contingency on equipment and cable"), manual accessory lines and labor lines have no equipment type, and a contingency over several subsystems cannot sit in one of the listed subsystems without distorting that subtotal. *Fix:* Add an explicit "unassigned / project-wide" group for lines with no equipment type and for percentage lines, and have the Check include it in the sum.

- **low** Additions to FR-7, FR-20, FR-29, FR-41 and FR-43 have no consequence in their Checks (lines 215–217, 223–225, 244–246, 495–499, 509–511) — For example FR-41 now says Changes "also lists cancellations and corrections under FR-55 against the current issued design" (line 244) but its Check (line 246) is unchanged; "against the current issued design" is also undefined for the new unissued-proposal cancellation and when FR-61 shows Not issued. *Fix:* Add one clause per Check, and say that cancellations of unissued proposals and corrections are listed in Changes regardless of issue state.

- **low** FR-65 leaves small state gaps (lines 559–567) — A warranty recorded before closeout has no start date yet ("defaults to that scope’s closeout date"); the text says "Until its start date, a recorded warranty is Not started" but not what is shown when the date itself does not exist. "An archived project is un-archived under FR-37 first" (line 567) does not say whether the single Start service visit action performs the un-archive or is blocked until the user does it, while line 94 requires un-archiving to be "explicit". *Fix:* State both.

- **low** The mistaken-mark reversal covers proposed devices only (line 585) — "A recorded installation or configuration mark made in error on a proposed device can be reversed ... does not apply to equipment surveyed or imported as existing." A mistaken done-mark for Relocate, Service/reconfigure or Remove on an Existing device has no stated correction path; a mistaken removal "archives the device in the same step" (line 108) and can only be undone by restoration under FR-54, which records a restoration and flags checks. *Fix:* State the intended path for these cases, even if it is "use restoration" or "record correction under FR-55".

## Scope honesty — adequate

Exclusions are explicit and were extended for the amendment (line 39: "risk-assessment records, specifications and bid documents, procurement, personnel management, project scheduling, elevation and detail drawings, service levels and spares, and backup-power design"). Each new FR closes with its own out-of-scope sentence (lines 354, 523, 569), and "After-POC candidates (not requirements)" (line 724) is honest de-scoping. The PRD does not use `[ASSUMPTION]` or `[NOTE FOR PM]` tags; given that it claims no open questions, the two findings under Decision-readiness about the amendment summary and line 712 are the scope-honesty risk. One further item:

### Findings

- **medium** The package-defining requirement FR-33 was not updated for content three other requirements send to it (lines 603–618; addendum.md lines 632–642) — FR-32 says the closeout record "also carries reliability runs under FR-64 and warranty records and service visits under FR-65" (line 539); FR-64 says "The Closeout package shows each run’s dates, planned and actual duration, downtime log, availability, target and outcome under FR-33" (line 533); FR-65 says warranty details appear "in the Closeout package" (line 561); FR-63 says "identify the resting view, previews and reach distinctly in analysis sheets under FR-33" (line 354). FR-33's Closeout preset row (line 608) and its analysis-sheet sentence ("Identify assessed camera views, unsupported panoramic outputs and illustrative footprints in analysis sheets", line 614) mention none of these, and line 714's list of pointer sentences ("FR-10/32/38/46/47/48") confirms FR-33 received none. The addendum's Package contents table is likewise unchanged. An extract of FR-33 alone yields a package without them. *Fix:* Add reliability runs, warranty records and service visits to the Closeout row, add resting view/preview/reach to the analysis-sheet sentence, and mirror both in the addendum table.

## Downstream usability — adequate

IDs are contiguous (FR-1 to FR-65, 65 Checks, no duplicates), cross-references in the new text resolve, and the device state model gives architecture a consolidated starting point with named values, which 1.0 lacked. The glossary did not keep pace with the amendment, and a few words now carry two meanings in places where the state model has made the distinction matter.

### Findings

- **medium** New domain nouns are not in the Terms table, and "view" now has two senses inside FR-63 (lines 52–70, 344–356) — The Terms table defines Camera view as an "Identified optical head/output beneath a camera asset" (line 56). FR-63 says a pan/tilt/zoom camera has "one view" (line 344), then calls the home position "the only view of this camera counted" (line 346) and refers to "every counted view" (line 356), which implies stored positions are also views. Home position, resting view, stored position, reach / reachable on demand, reliability period, stage, run, downtime entry, installation versus manufacturer warranty, service visit, service scope, un-archiving, cancellation and correction are all used as defined terms but appear only inside their FRs. *Fix:* Add rows for these, and state that a stored position is a setting of the single view, not a Camera view.

- **medium** "Return to service", "reopen" and "stage" are each used in two senses (lines 84, 246, 473, 521, 579, 589; addendum.md line 624) — "Return to service" means a Faulty device made Working in FR-41 ("a planned return to service is separately labeled", line 246) and a Removed device restored in FR-54 ("Actual return to service/relocation receives appropriate checks", line 579) and FR-55 (line 589); FR-28's table row (line 473) covers both. The state model now separates Condition from Lifecycle (lines 104–106), and it has no planned-action value for a planned return to service, so FR-41's label has no defined source. "Reopen" is redefined at line 94 and FR-37 to mean starting new work, yet FR-54 still says "Reopen an archived project under its existing policy before editing it" (line 579; same in addendum.md line 624), which now means un-archive. "Stage" is a workflow point at line 84 and a part of a reliability period at line 521. *Fix:* Change line 579 and addendum.md line 624 to "Un-archive"; use "restoration" for the lifecycle sense and reserve "return to service" for condition (or define both); rename FR-64's stages or add a Terms note.

- **low** The Planned action term row and the state model disagree on the value list (lines 61, 118, 233–240) — Terms: "retain, relocate, service/reconfigure, replace, remove, or add" (six, "add"). State model: "None, Retain, Relocate, Service/reconfigure, Replace, Remove, Add new" (seven). It is also not stated which action the successor record of a Replace carries; by the combination rules (lines 139–140) it can only be Add new. *Fix:* Align line 61 and say so for the successor.

## Shape fit — strong

Capability-spec shape with four organising journeys suits a single-practitioner tool at the top of a planning chain, and the brownfield discipline is kept: line 37 separates prototype capability from new requirements, and addendum.md line 833 notes "The prototype stores a single existing/proposed flag and has none of the other axes". Placing the device state model under Terms, with "Storage and representation belong to architecture" (line 98), is the right altitude. No findings.
